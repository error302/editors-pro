# Editors Pro monetisation proposal

Date: 1 November 2026. Policy sources checked on this date. Proposal only: this document adds no billing, paywall, entitlement checks or configured prices.

## Recommendation

Launch a useful free editor first, then test optional, one-time creative packs. Keep import, basic photo/video adjustments, timeline editing, text, songs, project save/reopen, preview and a useful verified export free. Keep the eight current looks free; sell additional work rather than charging again for the repaired baseline. Avoid interruptive ads while editing or exporting.

The current source has eight filter-based looks with intensity, bypass and reset. That provides a concrete starting point for extra recipe packs. It does not establish a film-stock simulation, AI enhancement or a complete standalone photo editor. Native modules or an export option in a menu are insufficient evidence of a sellable feature.

Before any purchase screen, demonstrate on the shipped Android build: imported video/photo/song; visible text and effects; audible preview; save/reopen retaining edits; playable exported output matching the edit, including audio; cancellation and missing-file recovery. Record the device, build and output. Purchases must not obscure or substitute for this acceptance work.

## Proposed offer

| Offer | Scope | Release condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Complete basic editing workflow, current looks, local projects and verified default export | Reliable import → edit → reopen → export on the release build |
| Optional look pack | Additional original recipes using supported filters, with accurate descriptions and intensity/bypass/reset; preview examples on real photos and video | Recipes differ meaningfully, preserve skin/neutral tones where promised, survive reopen and match export |
| Optional export upgrade | Selected higher-quality profiles or convenience controls already implemented and proven useful | Validate each exact resolution/FPS/codec/audio combination and target-device performance; do not sell a generic “4K/HDR/pro” promise from settings alone |

Do not charge for speculative cloud backup, AI transcription, stabilization, LUT workflows, film grain or batch processing until their complete customer workflows and ongoing costs are verified. Restoring ownership and opening existing projects must remain dependable; never silently remove a paid effect from a saved edit.

## One-time purchase versus subscription

Start with a non-consumable look pack, or a clearly scoped one-time tools bundle. Google documents non-consumables as permanent, account-associated benefits. Describe exactly what is included; avoid an unlimited “lifetime everything” promise covering future cloud costs or products. [Google: one-time products](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/one-time-products)

A one-time offer is straightforward for occasional editors but produces less predictable revenue and must fund maintenance. A subscription can fund continuing value, but adds recurring expectations and entitlement/cancellation work. Consider it later only for an actual sustained service or ongoing tool/content access that users repeatedly value. A static one-off pack should use a one-time product: Play requires sustained or recurring subscription value and prohibits disguising one-time benefits as subscriptions. [Google: subscriptions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533?hl=en)

## Price experiments and presentation

Hypothetical research cells, **not established market prices**: test one additional look pack at KES 199 versus KES 299 once, or a separately defined tools bundle at KES 999 versus KES 1,499 once. These are alternative interview/usability-test scenarios, not a commitment to charge, a currency-conversion rule or an instruction to show different prices secretly. Validate willingness to pay and support costs before selecting an offer; no subscription price is proposed yet.

Use the actual localized Play purchase price. State the full charge and included features before checkout. For any future subscription, prominently state billing period, renewal, post-trial price and cancellation access; show an annual total more prominently than a monthly equivalent. Make continuing free clear. Play requires accurate pricing/terms and matching app/billing prices. [Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en), [subscription disclosures](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533?hl=en)

## Billing policy and unit economics

Paid looks and export-feature unlocks are digital in-app goods. For a Play-distributed app, plan to use Google Play Billing. Alternative billing or external-payment links require an applicable policy exception/program and its conditions; do not assume a website, Stripe or M-Pesa link is automatically permitted. [Google: Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en)

Do **not** forecast a flat worldwide 15% fee. Google's current table distinguishes EEA/UK/US transactions from 30 June 2026 and Australia/Japan from 30 September 2026, including install cohorts, programs and billing fees. Its remaining-markets table lists enrolled developers at 15% on the first USD 1M annually, 30% above that, and 15% for auto-renewing subscriptions. Kenya is outside the five named rollout markets on this checked page; that is an inference from its published list, not confirmation of this developer account's rate. Recheck before launch. [Google: current service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en)

Tier enrollment requires a payments profile, the appropriate Account Group/associated-account declarations and acceptance of the tier terms. Associated-account earnings affect eligibility; Editors Pro's enrollment has not been checked. [Google: enrollment and account grouping](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10632485?hl=en)

Model contribution as receipts less applicable platform/billing fees, taxes/refunds, licensed assets and support/service costs. Compare successful first exports, repeat editing and interview willingness to pay before optimizing conversion. Any later billing implementation needs purchase verification, restore, pending/refunded purchases and offline entitlement behavior tested separately from editing.
